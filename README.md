# Fast Start 2

A balanced fast-start mod for Factorio, designed primarily for **Space Exploration** runs but fully compatible with vanilla.

Space Exploration is one of the most rewarding overhaul mods available — but the early game is a long grind that can kill a run before it gets interesting. This mod gets you past the repetitive bootstrapping phase so you can spend your time on the parts that actually matter.

Everything is configurable. If you want a lighter touch or a heavier one, dial it in via **Settings → Mod Settings → Map**.

---

## What You Get

On your **first join only**, the mod delivers three things:

### 🗺️ Radar Scan
The map is revealed in a large radius around your spawn point (default: 448 tiles, roughly 2× the diameter of a standard radar's scan area). The reveal is shared with your entire team in multiplayer. Radius is configurable.

### 📦 Starting Inventory
A set of mid-game infrastructure items so you can skip straight to building a real factory:

| Category | Items |
|---|---|
| Belts | Red belts, underground belts, splitters |
| Power | Medium electric poles, boilers, steam engines, offshore pumps |
| Mining | Electric mining drills |
| Smelting | Stone furnaces |
| Production | Blue assemblers (assembling-machine-2), fast inserters |

All quantities are individually configurable in Mod Settings.

### 🤖 Bots & Armor
Power armor pre-equipped with:
- **Fusion reactor** — infinite personal power, no batteries or solar needed
- **Personal roboports** — deploy construction bots immediately
- **Exoskeletons** — full movement speed from the start
- **Construction robots** — loaded into your inventory, ready to go

Defaults to **power armor mk2** (recommended for Space Exploration). Can be switched to mk1 in settings for vanilla runs where mk2 is a late-game unlock.

---

## Multiplayer

Each player receives their own kit independently on their first join. Returning players are tracked by a persistent save flag — rejoining a session will never give you the kit a second time.

---

## Configuration

All three feature groups can be independently enabled or disabled:

- **Enable starting inventory** — toggle the buildings/infrastructure kit
- **Enable radar scan** — toggle the map reveal
- **Enable construction bots & armor** — toggle the armor/equipment/bot bundle

Fine-grained quantity settings are available for every item category. See **Settings → Mod Settings → Map** in-game.

---

## Compatibility

- ✅ Space Exploration (primary target)
- ✅ Vanilla Factorio
- ✅ Multiplayer / dedicated servers
- ⚠️ Other mods that give starter items may stack with this one — disable the relevant feature groups in settings if needed

---

## Why "Fast Start 2"?

There are a few older fast-start mods around, most of which are unmaintained and have bugs (commonly: re-giving items every time you join a session). This is a clean rewrite with proper persistent state tracking, full configurability, and active maintenance.
